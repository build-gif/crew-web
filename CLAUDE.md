# Crew of Builders

Crew of Builders is the official marketing website for a curated community of founders, operators, and builders powered by **WE Heart Impact**. The site connects people who are actively building companies — sharing what they ship, learning from each other, and accessing mentorship, capital, and opportunities.

**Stack:** Next.js 16 App Router, TypeScript/JSX, Tailwind CSS v4, API routes for external integrations (Supabase, Resend, Notion).
